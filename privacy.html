Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: you can use YourNEETBuddy without an account, and then your study data stays in your own browser. If you sign in, we store your study data in your private account so it syncs across your devices. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

YourNEETBuddy (yourneetbuddy.com) is a free study companion for students preparing for NEET (UG), designed and developed by Raushan Raj ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

2. Using the app without an account

As a guest, everything you create (tasks, study hours, notes, quiz and mock-test history, goals, reminders, chapter progress and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see it. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Settings → Export backup to keep a copy.

3. Information we collect when you sign in

  • Account details from Google (or the email address you use for an email sign-in link): your name, email address and profile picture, and a unique account ID. We use these only to sign you in and show your profile.
  • Your study data: the same data described above, saved to your account so it is available on every device you use.

We never ask for or store your Google password. Sign-in is handled by Google and our authentication provider, Supabase.

4. AI features

  • When you generate an AI quiz, AI study notes or ask a doubt, the request (for example the subject, chapter, topic, your settings, and the doubt you typed) is sent to Google's Gemini AI service through our server, which applies a daily usage limit.
  • On Google's free tier, Google may use these inputs to improve its products. Please don't include personal information in quiz material or doubts.
  • If you add your own Gemini or OpenAI API key in Settings, requests go directly from your browser to that provider under your own account and its terms. Your key stays on your device and is never uploaded to our servers or included in backups.
  • The offline chapter notes and chapter tests are built into the app and need no AI or internet request.
  • The Resources page and chapter pages link to other websites (such as NCERT and NTA), which have their own privacy policies.

5. Usage limits and IP addresses

To keep the free AI fair for everyone, we count AI requests per day: by account ID for signed-in users, and by IP address for guests. These daily counters contain no other information and are used only to enforce limits.

6. How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, saving and syncing your data, and AI features.
  • To keep the service working and prevent abuse (for example, daily AI limits).

We do not sell or rent your data, show advertising, build advertising profiles, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

7. Where your data is stored and who processes it

We rely on these service providers, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase: authentication, database (your account and study data) and the server that calls the AI.
  • Google: Google sign-in and the Gemini AI service. Fonts are also loaded from Google Fonts.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the open-source Supabase code library used for sign-in.

Like any website, these providers may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load the site. Your data may be processed in India or other countries where these providers operate.

8. Storage on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage to save your data, your settings and, if you sign in, your login session. This is needed for the app to work and is not used for tracking.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. In our database, access rules ensure each signed-in user can read and change only their own data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

10. How long we keep data

  • Your account and study data are kept until you delete them.
  • Daily AI usage counters are kept only as long as needed to enforce limits and are cleared periodically.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Export backup downloads all your data as a file.
  • Correct: edit your profile and data in the app at any time.
  • Delete cloud data: Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my data from the cloud.
  • Delete your account entirely, or ask any privacy question: contact us (see below) and we'll act on it promptly.
  • Sign out at any time. This removes your data from that device while keeping it in your account.

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have further rights such as withdrawing consent or raising a grievance. Contact us to exercise them.

12. Students under 18

Many NEET aspirants are under 18. Everything in YourNEETBuddy works without an account, so no personal data is collected. If you are under 18, please create an account only with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see or delete a child's data (see Contact below), and we will do so promptly. We never use anyone's data for advertising or tracking.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll show a notice in the app.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or grievances, email yourneetbuddy1@gmail.com, or contact Raushan Raj via raushanraj.in.
